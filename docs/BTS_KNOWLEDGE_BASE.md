## Executive Summary

This knowledge base consolidates all coursework from the 4-semester BTS program in Stock Management & Logistics. The curriculum covers the complete supply chain lifecycle from information gathering and supplier selection to accounting controls and strategic logistics planning.

**Key Competencies Acquired:**
- Market research and information systems (Marketing Information System - SIM)
- Supplier sourcing, selection, and negotiation
- Procurement accounting and inventory control
- Strategic logistics planning and supply chain management

## Semester 1: Identification et Collection des Informations Internes et Externes

### Main Topics
1. **Marketing Information System (SIM)**
2. **Data Collection Methods** (qualitative & quantitative)
3. **Internal vs External Sources**
4. **Competitive Intelligence**
5. **Market Research Methodology**

### Key Concepts & Definitions

#### Marketing Information System (SIM)
> "Un réseau complet de relations structurées ou intervenant des hommes, des machines et des procédures qui a pour objet de engendrer un flux ordonné d'informations pertinentes, provenant des sources internes et externes à l'entreprise et destinées à servir de base aux décisions marketing" - Demers & Spicino

#### Information Quality Criteria
| Criterion | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Objective** | Impartial, verifiable through cross-checking |
| **Pertinent** | Relevant to the business and research objectives |
| **Precise & Reliable** | Verifiable source, trustworthy |
| **Recent** | Up-to-date, not obsolete |
| **Available** | Easily accessible for decision-making |

#### Forms of Information
- **Littérale (Literal):** Reports, press clippings, advertisements, web pages
- **Chiffrée (Numerical):** Dashboards, statistics, graphs
- **Support Types:** Verbal, written, IT, audiovisual

#### Data Sources
**Primary Data:** New data collected specifically for the research purpose (surveys, focus groups, interviews)

**Secondary Data:** Data already collected for another purpose
- *Internal Sources:* Customer files, supplier files, sales statistics, accounting documents
- *External Sources:* OECD, ONM, trade chambers, specialized publications, internet

### Research Methodology

#### Process Steps
1. Problem definition → Hypotheses formulation
2. Research framework design
3. Information collection and processing
4. Analysis and synthesis
5. Decision implementation

#### Types of Research
| Type | Purpose | Methods |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Exploratory** | Discover new phenomena | Focus groups, interviews |
| **Descriptive** | Describe characteristics | Surveys, panels |
| **Causal** | Explain cause-effect | Experiments, tests |

### Qualitative Studies
- **Motivation Studies:** Understanding consumer motivations through non-directive interviews
- **Focus Groups:** Group discussions (6-8 participants) to explore perceptions
- **Projective Techniques:** Word association, sentence completion, thematic perception tests
- **Delphi Method:** Expert consensus through iterative rounds

### Quantitative Studies
- **Survey Design:** Questionnaire creation, sampling methods
- **Data Collection:** CAWI (Computer Assisted Web Interviewing), telephone, face-to-face
- **Statistical Analysis:** Cross-tabulations, percentages, indices

### Competitive Intelligence
**Definition:** Monitoring competitors' strategies, innovations, and market movements.

#### Performance Indicators
**Commercial Performance:**
- Market share (value & volume)
- Brand image perception
- Customer satisfaction

**Financial Performance:**
- Net result = Products - Charges
- Commercial margin = Sales - Cost of goods sold
- Profitability analysis

#### Competitive Tools
- **Veille Concurrentielle:** Monitoring competitor activities
- **Benchmarking:** Comparing best practices
- **CRM (GRC):** Customer Relationship Management
- **CKM (GCC):** Customer Knowledge Management

### Environmental Analysis
**Micro-economic Environment:** Customers, suppliers, banks, employees, competitors, unions, government

**Macro-economic Environment (PESTEL):**
- **P**olitical: Laws, regulations
- **E**conomic: Demographics, purchasing power
- **S**ocio-cultural: Consumer habits, trends
- **T**echnological: Innovation, automation
- **E**nvironmental: Sustainability, ecology
- **L**egal: Quality standards, certifications

### Connection to Inventory Management
- Information systems support demand forecasting
- Supplier data analysis improves procurement decisions
- Customer data enables better stock planning
- Competitive intelligence informs pricing and stock mix

## Semester 2: Recherche et Sélection des Fournisseurs

### Main Topics
1. **Supplier Sourcing Process**
2. **Request for Quotation (RFQ) Procedures**
3. **Supplier Evaluation Criteria**
4. **Negotiation Techniques**
5. **Contract Management**

### Key Concepts & Definitions

#### Sourcing Definition
> "L'acte qui vise à réduire le coût général des achats, en automatisant les processus concernés."

**Sourcing Objectives:**
- Identify and develop international suppliers
- Expand supplier panel
- Increase competition among suppliers
- Continuous price and quality comparison
- Prevent monopolies
- Discover innovations

#### Sourcing Process (3 Stages)
1. **Identification:** Research potential suppliers in target countries
2. **Collection:** Gather information from suppliers, embassies, customs
3. **Cartography:** Create supplier map with all collected information

### Request for Quotation (RFQ) Process

#### RFQ Documents
| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Introduction letter | Context and objectives |
| Acknowledgment of receipt | Supplier confirmation |
| RFQ guide | Rules and procedures |
| Specifications | Product/service requirements |
| Terms and conditions | Business relationship framework |
| Questionnaire | General supplier information |
| Response guide | Forms, deadlines, formats |
| Annexes | Additional documentation |

#### Electronic RFQ (e-Sourcing)
- Online supplier consultation
- Automated offer submission
- Time-saving analysis tools
- Real-time tracking

### Offer Analysis Process

#### Stage 1: Pre-selection
- Eliminatory criteria (technical competence, equipment, location)
- Compliance with specifications

#### Stage 2: Multi-criteria Analysis
| Criterion | Evaluation Factors |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Quality** | Product quality, certifications (ISO), management systems |
| **Cost** | Acquisition cost, price competitiveness, payment terms |
| **Delivery** | Lead times, reliability, storage capability |
| **Other** | Financial stability, adaptability, reputation |

### Supplier Selection Formulas

#### Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)
```
TCO = Purchase Price + Transaction Costs + Acquisition Costs + 
      Operating Costs + End-of-Life Costs
```

#### Weighted Score Method
```
Score = Σ (Criterion Weight × Supplier Rating) / Total Weight
```

#### Cost Comparison Formula
```
Unit Cost = (Purchase Price + Freight + Insurance + Customs) / Quantity
```

### Negotiation Process

#### Three Phases
1. **Information Collection:** Supplier situation, market position, production capacity
2. **Technical Preparation:** Identify negotiation points, define objectives, prepare arguments
3. **Contractualization:** Establish purchase agreement with agreed terms

#### Contract Elements
- Parties identification (names, addresses)
- Object of contract (product/service)
- Quality specifications (annexed cahier des charges)
- Price, quantity, delivery dates
- Penalties for delays
- Warranty terms
- Dispute resolution jurisdiction

### Supplier Evaluation Matrix

| Category | Criteria | Weight | Score (1-5) | Weighted Score |
|----------|----------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Quality | Product quality | 25% | | |
| | Certifications | 15% | | |
| Cost | Price competitiveness | 20% | | |
| | Payment terms | 10% | | |
| Delivery | Lead time | 15% | | |
| | Reliability | 10% | | |
| Service | After-sales support | 5% | | |
| **Total** | | **100%** | | |

### Connection to Inventory Management
- Supplier selection impacts stock quality and costs
- Negotiation affects purchase prices and storage costs
- Delivery reliability influences safety stock levels
- Contract terms determine reorder quantities

## Semester 3: Enregistrement Comptable et Contrôle des Approvisionnements

### Main Topics
1. **Procurement Accounting**
2. **Inventory Valuation Methods**
3. **Stock Control Systems**
4. **Budget Management**
5. **Cost Analysis**

### Key Concepts & Definitions

#### Accounting Principles for Inventory
- **Matching Principle:** Costs matched to revenue generation
- **Prudence Principle:** Conservative valuation
- **Consistency Principle:** Same methods over time
- **Materiality Principle:** Focus on significant items

#### Inventory Valuation Methods

| Method | Formula | When to Use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **FIFO** (First-In-First-Out) | Oldest stock sold first | Perishable goods |
| **LIFO** (Last-In-First-Out) | Newest stock sold first | Inflation periods |
| **Weighted Average** | Total cost ÷ Total units | Bulk commodities |
| **Specific Identification** | Actual cost per item | Unique/expensive items |

#### Key Accounting Terms

**Marge Commerciale (Commercial Margin):**
```
Marge Commerciale = Ventes de marchandises - Coût d'achat des marchandises vendues
```

**Variation de Stock:**
```
Variation = Stock Final - Stock Initial
```

**Coût d'Acquisition:**
```
Coût = Prix d'achat + Frais accessoires + Droits de douane - Ristournes
```

**CMUP (Coût Moyen Unitaire Pondéré):**
```
CMUP = Valeur totale stock / Quantité totale stock
```

### Stock Control Systems

#### ABC Analysis (Pareto Principle)
| Class | % Items | % Value | Control Level |
|-------|---------|---------|---------------|
| **A** | 10-20% | 70-80% | Tight control, frequent review |
| **B** | 20-30% | 15-25% | Moderate control |
| **C** | 50-70% | 5-10% | Simple control, bulk ordering |

#### Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)
```
EOQ = √(2DS / H)
```
Where:
- D = Annual demand
- S = Ordering cost per order
- H = Holding cost per unit per year

#### Reorder Point (ROP)
```
ROP = (Daily usage × Lead time) + Safety stock
```

#### Safety Stock Formula
```
Safety Stock = Z × σ × √L
```
Where:
- Z = Service level factor (e.g., 1.65 for 95%)
- σ = Standard deviation of demand
- L = Lead time

### Stock Turnover Analysis

**Inventory Turnover Ratio:**
```
Turnover = Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory
```

**Days Sales of Inventory (DSI):**
```
DSI = (Average Inventory / COGS) × 365
```

**Stock Coverage Ratio:**
```
Coverage = Current Stock / Average Daily Usage
```

### Budget Management

#### Annual Purchase Budget
```
Budget = Σ (Forecasted quantity × Unit cost) + Contingency
```

#### Variance Analysis
```
Variance = Actual Cost - Budgeted Cost
Price Variance = (Actual Price - Standard Price) × Actual Quantity
Quantity Variance = (Actual Quantity - Standard Quantity) × Standard Price
```

### Connection to Inventory Management
- Accounting methods directly impact stock valuation
- EOQ optimization reduces total inventory costs
- ABC analysis prioritizes management attention
- Budget control prevents overstocking/understocking

## Semester 4: Planification Stratégique de la Logistique

### Main Topics
1. **Strategic Logistics Planning**
2. **Supply Chain Management**
3. **Distribution Strategy**
4. **Performance Measurement**
5. **Sustainability in Logistics**

### Key Concepts & Definitions

#### Logistics Strategy Definition
> "L'ensemble des décisions à long terme qui influencent la capacité de l'entreprise à servir ses clients de manière efficace et efficiente."

#### Supply Chain Management (SCM)
"Conception, coordination et exécution du flux optimisé de biens, services et informations du point d'origine au point de consommation."

### Strategic Planning Framework

#### 1. Vision and Mission
- Define long-term logistics objectives
- Align with corporate strategy
- Set performance targets

#### 2. SWOT Analysis
| Internal | External |
|----------|----------|
| **Strengths:** Core competencies | **Opportunities:** Market growth |
| **Weaknesses:** Resource gaps | **Threats:** Competition |

#### 3. Strategic Objectives
- Cost reduction targets
- Service level improvements
- Sustainability goals
- Technology adoption roadmap

### Distribution Strategy Models

#### Centralized Distribution
- Single warehouse serves all regions
- Lower inventory costs
- Higher transportation costs
- Better control

#### Decentralized Distribution
- Multiple regional warehouses
- Lower transportation costs
- Higher inventory costs
- Faster delivery

#### Hybrid Model
- Combination of centralized and decentralized
- Balance cost and service
- Optimize based on product characteristics

### Performance Metrics

#### Logistics Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Category | KPI | Formula |
|----------|-----|---------|
| **Cost** | Logistics cost ratio | Logistics costs / Revenue |
| | Transportation cost per unit | Total transport cost / Units shipped |
| **Service** | Order fill rate | Orders shipped complete / Total orders |
| | On-time delivery | On-time deliveries / Total deliveries |
| **Efficiency** | Inventory turnover | COGS / Average inventory |
| | Warehouse utilization | Used space / Total space |
| **Quality** | Damage rate | Damaged items / Total items |
| | Return rate | Returns / Total sales |

### Supply Chain Optimization

#### Total Logistics Cost Model
```
Total Cost = Transportation + Warehousing + Inventory Carrying + 
             Order Processing + Stockout Costs
```

#### Service Level Optimization
```
Service Level = 1 - (Stockouts / Total Demand)
```

#### Fill Rate Calculation
```
Fill Rate = (Items shipped from stock / Total items ordered) × 100%
```

### Distribution Network Design

#### Network Optimization Factors
1. **Customer locations** and demand patterns
2. **Product characteristics** (value, weight, perishability)
3. **Service requirements** (delivery time, frequency)
4. **Cost structure** (transport, warehousing, inventory)

#### Facility Location Methods
- **Center of Gravity Method:** Find optimal location based on weighted distances
- **Load-Distance Method:** Minimize total transportation cost
- **Break-even Analysis:** Compare location alternatives

### Sustainability in Logistics

#### Green Logistics Principles
1. **Reduce:** Minimize packaging, optimize routes
2. **Reuse:** Returnable containers, recycled materials
3. **Recycle:** Sustainable packaging, reverse logistics

#### Carbon Footprint Calculation
```
CO2 = Distance × Weight × Emission Factor
```

### Technology in Logistics

#### Key Technologies
- **WMS (Warehouse Management System):** Inventory tracking, optimization
- **TMS (Transportation Management System):** Route planning, carrier selection
- **ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning):** Integrated business management
- **IoT (Internet of Things):** Real-time tracking, monitoring
- **AI/ML:** Demand forecasting, predictive analytics

### Connection to Inventory Management
- Strategic planning determines inventory policies
- Distribution network affects stock positioning
- Performance metrics drive inventory optimization
- Technology enables real-time inventory visibility

## Formulas & Calculations Reference

### Inventory Formulas

| Formula | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **CMUP** = Total Value / Total Quantity | Weighted Average Unit Cost |
| **Stock Final** = Stock Initial + Entrées - Sorties | Ending Inventory |
| **Rotation** = COGS / Average Inventory | Turnover Ratio |
| **Couverture** = Stock / Daily Consumption | Days of Supply |
| **Taux de rupture** = Ruptures / Demande | Stockout Rate |
| **Valeur du stock** = Quantité × CMUP | Inventory Value |

### Financial Formulas

| Formula | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Marge** = Ventes - Coût d'achat | Margin |
| **Résultat** = Produits - Charges | Net Result |
| **Rentabilité** = Résultat / Chiffre d'affaires | Profitability |
| **Seuil de rentabilité** = Charges fixes / Taux marge | Break-even Point |

### Supply Chain Formulas

| Formula | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **EOQ** = √(2DS/H) | Economic Order Quantity |
| **ROP** = (Demand × Lead Time) + Safety Stock | Reorder Point |
| **Safety Stock** = Z × σ × √L | Buffer Stock |
| **TCO** = Purchase + Transaction + Operating Costs | Total Cost of Ownership |

### Performance Formulas

| Formula | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Taux de service** = 1 - (Ruptures/Demande) | Service Level |
| **Taux de satisfaction** = Commandes livrées / Total | Fill Rate |
| **Coût logistique** = Total coûts / CA | Logistics Cost Ratio |
