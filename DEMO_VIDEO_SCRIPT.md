## Recording Instructions

### Tools Needed
- **Screen recording:** Xbox Game Bar (Win + G) or OBS Studio
- **Video editing:** Clipchamp (free) or DaVinci Resolve

### Recording Steps
1. Open `https://academix-dss-hardware.streamlit.app/1_Tableau_de_Bord`
2. Press `Win + G` to open Xbox Game Bar
3. Click Record
4. Follow the script above
5. Stop recording after 60 seconds
6. Edit in Clipchamp (add text overlays, music)

### Tips
- Keep it under 60 seconds
- Use upbeat background music
- Add text overlays for each feature
- End with clear CTA (contact info)
- Export as MP4, 1080p
